§3.1 Week 1 · Connect Jira → unlock ROI measurement

Data source gap. Jira is not yet integrated in the dashboard pipeline. AI spend, Git, and Cursor / Anthropic / OpenAI telemetry are live; Jira (and PR review data) are the only missing pieces. Until Jira ingestion lands, ROI metrics that depend on closed-ticket counts are baseline-only and cannot be tracked over time.
ROI metric 1
$ / closed-Jira ?Sum of AI spend by author × month divided by the number of Jira tickets that author closed in the same window. Requires the Jira integration. Target trend: −20% by month 3.
ROI metric 2
$ / merged-PR ?Sum of AI spend by author × month divided by merged PRs (Git telemetry). Already computable today. Distinguishes "AI active" from "AI productive".
ROI metric 3
$ / passing-test ?Sum of AI spend by author × month divided by tests added that pass CI. Already computable from CI artefacts. Should rise as the test-floor policy lands.
